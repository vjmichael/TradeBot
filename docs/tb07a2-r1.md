# TB-07A2-R1 — recovery-only paper protection and verified release

## Bounded candidate

The installed desk is still PAPER/PAUSED. This candidate does not authorize RUN, initiate new entries from the scheduler, provision native workspaces, execute live orders or enable a service. The protection worker is independently callable by a trusted supervisor for an existing registered paper workspace and bound intent. It holds an owner-only OS file lock on the native workspace; a Linux recovery-only systemd template provides restart-on-failure, bounded startup/shutdown, heartbeat watchdog, memory/process bounds and no signing credentials. The worker constructs a fixed dry-run configuration without environment overlays and no autonomous entry loop. Only public-market price reads are intended, no exchange private credentials. Same-UID writers and exclusive native ownership outside the cooperative service remain a deployment trust boundary. Native workspace initialization, integrated protected entry ownership and installed service health reporting are not qualified.

The worker polls exact native positions and outstanding orders, evaluates a fresh bid against immutable approved stop/target, cancels a still-open entry remainder on pause/expiry/protection trigger before a sell, and uses native execute_trade_exit with a guarded, bounded spot sell only. The stop/target remains effective after desk pause or expired entry authority. After a terminal partial exit, the original protection reason persists and the exact remaining position can be sold, even if the price returns inside the band. An exit attempt is committed before native submission; an absent tagged native result is ambiguous and cannot be blindly retried. A stale quote or unknown native state blocks execution and never releases funds. Watchdog restart is not a guarantee of uninterrupted price availability or successful liquidation. No guaranteed stop fill price.

The financial reconciler admits one terminal buy receipt and multiple distinct terminal sell receipts attributed to recorded exit tags, validates USD fees and exact fixed-point quantities/cost, retains a full hold while any part is unresolved, and records each terminal partial receipt only once. Open cumulative partials remain encumbered pending a terminal observation rather than guessed incremental fee allocation. Native wallet quote/base (native-simulator ledger, independent of Maj Vaj ledger, not an independent venue) must match total cash and pending proceeds at complete close. A terminal close requires no remaining quantity or pending sale discrepancy. Settlement and an explicit execution_closed financial event are in the same transaction as the native_released event, bound to its digest, native view and workspace. Gate/reserve/accounting exclude only the paired verified release, do not call it NO_FILL, and require renewed whole-desk reconciliation before admission. Old readers retain the historical FILLED_HELD state; new Gate decision presentation reports ACCOUNTED for matched release. Older financial replay rejects the new kind rather than interpreting it as monetary authority. No schema migration. Legacy TB-07A2 imports and missing no-fill evidence remain held for manual review. Missing fee evidence can prevent accounting without blocking a verified risk-reducing sell; failed native observation or ambiguous exit requires recovery review.
