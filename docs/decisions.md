## TB-04C partition storage decision

Partition published raw market revisions by pair/timeframe and UTC daily (fine) or epoch-aligned 30-day (coarse) block; keep SQLite source/revision journal authoritative until source retention and whole-store recovery qualification. Manifest epoch changes atomically with source visibility and candle revisions; query verified current Parquet plus SQLite for dirty/unmaterialized blocks, with as-of and conflict semantics unchanged. No silently successful query of invalid active partitions. Derived lake byte quota and disk reserve admission, read-only user-visible disk status, explicit dry-run/apply retired-unpinned retention and orphan recovery, no autonomous removal of source observations. Whole-store backup/restore and hard raw-storage retention policy remain release gates. TB-04B native bridge failure remains separately open; no relaxation of independent boundary checks.

## TB-FIN-01 financial protection decisions

Default protect 100% of positive settled realized gains net of trading fees; no automatic reinvestment or recovery of losses from reserves.
