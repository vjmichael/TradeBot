# TB-FIN-01C — protected profit policy foundation

Status: implementation candidate, PAPER only. This is an internal deterministic financial policy, not a dashboard approval broker, deployed scheduler, live execution adapter or actual human authorization. OFF and 0% released by default. There is deliberately no runtime default key.

Every verified positive net settled gain remains protected. The reserve ledger classifies that balance into unallocated, estimated tax, household, emergency savings and other savings. A human-approved classification moves no money outside the account and does not affect P/L. A release is a journaled reclassification from protected to active cash, never a new deposit or profit. Current approved allocation plus released credits cannot exceed the operator's separately configured desk cap. The Gate includes only ledger-verified released credits, and no remaining protected balance.

The internal reserve manager keeps immutable versioned saved policy separate from active approval, and preview is read-only. Activation verifies a short-lived dedicated approval signature binding the exact version, policy, desk journal head, paper portfolio and receipt ID. Exact one-time release similarly binds amount, floor and capital ceiling. Revocation is version-bound and can proceed across an intervening observation. The approval key is injected by a future dedicated human-authentication broker; no route or scheduler is installed. The active policy is an immutable snapshot, unaffected by edits or saves. Standing approval requires an explicit rate, fixed-dollar/percentage/combined floor, cap, per-cycle/lifetime limits, UTC anchor and start/end/expiry. The 90-day maximum duration and five-minute execution window are conservative implementation bounds to review.

At a newly eligible 24-hour cycle: calculate cumulative positive settled gains minus earmarks and floor; deduct the previous evaluated high-water cursor; apply rate; round down to cents; clamp to protected unallocated cash above floor, desk and portfolio headroom and approval budgets. Advance both cursor and cycle even on skips; skip missed intervals rather than catching up. No current surplus is automatically included at activation. An exact one-time authorization is a separate mechanism. Require recent independently reconciled balances and no unresolved Gate holds, hard pause, pending settlements, financial conflict, correction lock or realized loss. The conservative lifetime gross loss lock purposely cannot automatically be reset. No external transfer or withdrawal; no market orders. After policy events obtain renewed reconciliation. No release while actual desk remains PAUSED; existing live path remains unavailable.

The event stream is chained with existing financial custody; release and disposition commit together. The per-instance released amount is a separately auditable credit, not a change to original contributed capital. Store cap allocation and status now count credits. Historical corrections after policy history are held for human review instead of replaying an unsafe automatic change. The approval broker, review/unlock flow, actual independent paper receipt collector, scheduled process, UI, and full integration acceptance remain outstanding. This candidate was locally tested with fixture approval signatures and real SQLite, not operator-authenticated runtime use.
