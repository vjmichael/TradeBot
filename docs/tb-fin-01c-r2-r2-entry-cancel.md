Earlier claims without the context cannot enter through this boundary.

A paused/expired pending PAPER buy is frozen from actual existing simulator state before any fill-generating poll. Missing simulator state is held, not inferred to be zero. Restart re-applies actual frozen proof only. No additional entry authority from a snapshot.
- Signed zero-fill terminal v3 transports the minimum cancellation proof and independently observed flat native wallet to the desk. The desk checks signature, original binding, journal prefix, exact intent, quantity/price bound, absence of earlier economic mutations, and exact wallet/financial agreement; atomically records NO_FILL and releases the unused reservation. Exact duplicates are nonmutating. Native retires its mailbox only on a fresh signed snapshot of the exact acknowledgment. No personal keys, raw order secrets or claim token transferred. Partial filled cancellations remain exposure, subject to normal protection/reconciliation.
