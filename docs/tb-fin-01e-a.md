# TB-FIN-01E-A — historical paper financial reports

Read-only reporting extension on top of FIN-01A/D/R1/R2. Financial Goals offers per-portfolio Financial history: 7, 14, 30 or 31 local calendar days ending at a selected date. Authenticated GET /api/financial-history/{instance}, days bounded 1–31, canonical YYYY-MM-DD no future cutoff, same existing reporting zone, explicit PAPER/virtual and no real income. No new authorization, execution, reserve, withdrawal, deposit, provider or notification action. No data egress and no new financial tables. Scope is a single registered paper instance; do not aggregate independent hypothetical comparison portfolios into household income or claim desk-wide native balance reconciliation.

Verify complete ledger chain before report and match funded scope to current registered identity. Replay ordered booked events through exclusive local midnight boundaries, verify nondecreasing posting timestamps and reject future/invalid clock. Snapshot historic booked balances from the prefix at each cutoff rather than today's state. Period totals and daily deltas for realized P/L, settled P/L, recorded trading fees and net reserve releases separately. Fees already used in realized P/L must not be deducted again; reserve transfers and principal funding are not trading profit. Closing active and protected cash, unsettled proceeds, remaining inventory cost basis, original principal and open-position indicator. No historical mark or native wallet value invented; never label cost basis as market equity. Realization and settlement can span days, and later corrections appear on posting day without silently rewriting prior booked records. Partial funding/current day, not-yet-funded and review-required rows explicit; totals only sum reported values, not missing days as zero; no populated rows => unavailable totals. Any known desk journal conflict or selected unresolved correction suppresses all requested money figures. Integrity or scope failure refuses the request. Report has exact journal head/count and as-of time, reporting dates and zone. Current journal conflicts may conservatively suppress otherwise reconstructable historical values. Still a booked posting-time report, not verified exchange statements, execution-time restatement, mark-to-market return, actual service cost or withdrawal report.
