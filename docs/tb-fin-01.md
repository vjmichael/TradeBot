# TB-FIN-01 — Financial goals and profit protection

**Date:** 2026-11-18 (Arizona)  
**Status:** Binding design expansion; implementation and acceptance pending.  
**Source:** TradeBot_TB-FIN-01_Design.docx, sections 1–6, plus the user's clarifications on net profits, non-reinvestment and daily dollar/point goals. This document supplements v0.7 and v0.8; it supersedes any conflicting implicit compounding. All existing paper-first, risk, authentication, no-withdrawal-key and live-authorization requirements remain binding.

## 1. Objective and governing rule

TradeBot helps the operator plan household finances and protect realized net trading gains. The default is 100% protection of positive realized net gains, immediately upon verified settlement, never automatic reinvestment. Only explicitly authorized principal is at risk; profits cannot increase order sizing, bankroll, strategy allocations or available trading cash unless explicitly released under the bounded human authorization below. Losses reduce active capital. No automatic replenishment from any reserve, new deposit, savings or other instance. Never increase risk or force trading to achieve a financial goal. All calculations and protection work without Grok or Cognitum.

## 2. Financial goals and daily points (TB-FIN-01A)

User-defined monthly expenses, other monthly take-home income, desired additional savings, authorized active capital, estimated tax reserve rate t (0 <= t < 1), USD reporting and IANA time zone (initial America/Phoenix). Nonnegative decimal inputs with validation. Monthly after-tax funding shortfall S = max(0, expenses + savings - other income). Monthly required pre-tax trading profit for planning = S / (1 - t); daily equivalent divides by actual days in that local calendar month. Example: $2,500 expenses + $500 savings - $1,500 income = $1,500 shortfall; 20% estimated reserve => $1,875 monthly pre-tax target, $62.50/day for a 30-day month. Separately show actual Grok, hosting and data costs; an optional cash-flow target can include expenses not already counted, but never imply tax deductibility or subtract twice.

Add optional user-set daily goals in both dollars and **portfolio return points**. Initial definition proposed for review: 1 point = 1 percentage point of day-start active capital; equivalently 100 basis points. Use a fixed beginning-of-day reference C, measured reconciled active equity attributable to authorized capital (excluding protected reserves, including marked active positions with valid prices); new deposits, withdrawals, reserve transfers and authorization changes are not trading profits and must not change the denominator retroactively. In paper mode use the virtual equivalent. Net realized trading P/L during the local day P includes gains, losses and fees matched to realized fills. Display realized goal progress in USD and points = 100*P/C. This is a normalized realized P/L metric, not total mark-to-market portfolio return. Also report unrealized and total active-equity changes separately. If C=0 or unknown, dollar P/L remains visible; point conversion unavailable.  
- User chooses a primary target in dollars or points; derive the other from C. Do not set conflicting automatic goals. Decimal rounding is display-only. Show an operator override versus the derived household daily plan separately. Neither is a trading quota. For $100 day-start capital and 2 points, daily target is $2. For $500 and the same target, $10. A $5 net realized gain on $100 is +5 points; a $3 loss is -3 points. An intraday reserve transfer is not a loss or profit, and cannot reset progress. Points are not raw BTC/ETH/SOL price ticks or a forecast. Optional raw asset-price movement can be labeled separately.
- Display local-day and month-to-date actual net P/L, target dollars/points, progress and shortfall, protected amount, estimated tax allocation, expenses/savings allocation, realized versus unrealized, cost and withdrawal status. Show goal reached notification; allow optional pause of **new entries** upon target (explicitly configured by operator, default off); existing protective exits and reconciliation always continue. Durable once-per-day trigger, preserve pauses across restart. No forced liquidation or trade to hit/avoid the target; goals reset by calendar day, not arbitrary session/withdrawal. No daily rollover of missed goals into increased stake. Daily and monthly goals are reference/planning metrics rather than performance promises.
- Account-wide progress uses a reconciled aggregate base without summing overlapping portfolio ownership. Comparison cohorts are independent virtual ledgers, not household cash. Never aggregate paper results as real income.
- Test calendar boundaries including leap year, DST, loss/zero targets, zero base, intraday deposits/reserve withdrawals, late fee corrections and restart, daily target notification/optional pause and no risk escalation. Store actual daily baselines and policy versions.

## 3. Durable financial accounting (TB-FIN-01B)

Balanced event journal or equivalent, fixed-point/Decimal, explicit currency and precision, idempotent venue/account/event IDs. Distinguish authorized capital ceiling, settled available active cash, pending-order reservations, open positions and lot cost basis, realized/unrealized P/L, pending/protected settled gains, reserve suballocations, deposits, withdrawals, estimated tax/operating costs and fees. Paper and live isolated; central account authority prevents double allocation across instances. Reconcile exchange balances, orders, fills, fees and transfers at startup and before admissions. Late or duplicate information requires append-only correction/reversal and deterministic reconstruction. No secrets in reporting.

Use a documented consistent lot-cost method; match entry and exit fees, partial fill amounts, actual proceeds, fee asset conversion with verified valuation, and avoid double-counting actual spread/slippage already implicit in fill prices. Unknown fees, unsettled proceeds or conflicts are unavailable for new entries until reconciled. No paper fill presented as actual. Unrealized gains, deposits, transfers and returned principal are not profit. Actual net trading P/L is distinct from after-operating-cost or after-tax cash flow.

Illustration: original principal $100; gross sale proceeds $205, aggregate trading fees $5; net realized profit $100, original active capital $100, protected profit $100, settled attributable value $200. Assumes no other positions, fees or obligations outstanding; tax, withdrawal and service costs separate. The example is accounting arithmetic, not expected returns.

## 4. Profit harvesting (TB-FIN-01C)

Protect every positive settled realized net gain, never reinvest below a notification threshold. Configurable thresholds ($ amount or 2/5/10/25% of explicitly authorized base) group alerts or withdrawal-readiness reporting, **not** permission to put unharvested gains at risk. Track cumulative gains, net losses, high-water mark, unrecovered losses, reserves and prior harvest IDs; once credited, do not count the same profit twice; late downward corrections reconcile actual balances, quarantine discrepancies and protect the remaining verified reserve. No automatic release of already protected money to restore losses, even after a drawdown. Positive gains after a loss remain protected by default; any restoration of active capital requires a separate explicit authorization. Closing a trade with a loss leaves active principal reduced, e.g., $100 active + $100 protected followed by $20 realized loss => $80 active, $100 protected. No reserve top-up.

Reserve allocation subaccounts (mutually exclusive): unallocated protected profit, estimated tax, household expenses, emergency savings, other savings. Transfer between subaccounts does not create income. All remain excluded from available trading and order reservations. Exclude from trading admission both accounting reserves and physical withdrawals; show pending transfers, failed transfers, fees and verified external receipts. Accounting segregation on exchange is not a guarantee of custody protection or physical segregation. Operator may withdraw manually via Kraken; no withdrawal-capable bot key or automated money movement. Any future transfer mechanism is separately qualified and explicitly authorized; this amendment authorizes none.

### Optional reinvestment widget — human-authorized, default disabled

Default reinvestment permission absent and rate 0%. The formerly illustrated 10% is an example, not a preset or recommendation. Support a one-time exact amount release, or a separate explicitly approved bounded standing policy for profit-only reinvestment. A scheduler may execute an already authorized policy but cannot create, extend, or broaden it. Require strong dashboard authentication, named account/portfolio and paper/live mode, explicit percentage, selected reserve floor mode, hard maximum total authorized trading capital, per-cycle and lifetime release caps, approval expiry, start/end and a preview of worst-case additional capital at risk. No inherited permission from paper to live, no indefinite authorization. Human can disable/revoke immediately; revocation prevents new reserve releases and new exposure outside the remaining approved cap, without abandoning protective exits. An increase, renewal, floor reduction, scope change or retrospective release requires fresh approval. All releases are auditable, atomic and idempotent. Default floor and ceiling unset; cannot enable until explicitly provided. No model, strategy, Telegram or external research adapter can authorize reinvestment.

Profit floor modes: fixed dollars, percentage of a documented cumulative settled-profit reference, or the greater of both. The reference is the account/portfolio's verified positive net profit credits through the cycle, less validated reversals, excluding deposits, principal, unrealized gains, fees already accounted for and non-trading transfers. Retain an audited nondecreasing floor reference within the approval so losses/withdrawals do not implicitly lower the floor; if the retained reserve is below it, release zero. A human may explicitly rebaseline subject to hard limits, with no double counting. Never calculate the rate or floor from total account balance, NAV, deposits or unrelated reserves. Earmarked tax, household expense, emergency and other explicitly protected savings and pending withdrawals remain ineligible regardless of floor or rate. A floor is an exclusion from entry sizing, not a guarantee against exchange/custody failure.

Evaluate at most once per fixed 24-hour cycle anchored to an approved UTC time (persist the cycle ID and cutoff; local Arizona calendar-day reporting remains separate). Market monitoring, eligible entries inside independently approved risk limits and protection continue 24/7 across Asia, Europe/UK and USA sessions. These sessions are analytics windows, not separate bankrolls or an automatic reset of limits. Initially no session-by-session automatic release; paper research may later compare daily/weekly/session policies without changing production approval. No goal or cycle boundary forces trades, pauses exits or resets risk locks.

At each cycle, settle and reconcile fees, fills, withdrawals and reserve ledger before considering release. Track each settled net positive gain once and a durable eligible-surplus high-water cursor. Calculate cumulative newly eligible incremental profit above the configured floor, excluding earmarked reserves, subtracting previous evaluated eligible surplus (not merely previous released amounts). The cursor never decreases automatically after a loss, correction, expiry, skip or withdrawal. Compute tentative release as rate times that incremental eligible surplus, rounded down to supported units. Clamp to verified unearmarked reserve surplus above floor, outstanding free settled funds, remaining per-cycle/lifetime authorization and hard active capital ceiling; do not increase an order merely to meet minimum notional. Any invalid/unknown inputs, conflicting data, insufficient reserve, stale/revoked consent or hard risk lock => no release. Never catch up skipped, missed, disabled or previously evaluated cycles automatically; record disposition and cursor transactionally with release. Existing surplus when enabling policy is excluded unless separately previewed and explicitly included in initial authorization. Positive profits are protected immediately before evaluation. Negative P/L still reduces active capital; a scheduled release must not automatically replenish trading losses, so freeze new releases after realized drawdown until separate human review/reapproval. No protection pool becomes negative or available solely because available cash or account value rises. Account-wide aggregate caps apply across instances and pending reservations.

Illustrative no-loss sequence, assuming $100 original authorized bankroll, $50 fixed floor, 10% chosen by the operator, sufficient remaining caps and no earmarks: $25 earned -> $0 release; $50 cumulative -> $0; $100 cumulative -> total $5 released, $95 protected, $105 active; $200 cumulative -> total $15 released, $185 protected, $115 active (only $10 additional release). If maximum bankroll is $105, no more is released. Default disabled means all $100 is protected and active remains $100. These are accounting examples, not returns or suggested settings. Test percentage and combined floors; floor/ceiling clamps; cumulative distinct events; multi-instance shared cap; settlement and correction; duplicate ticks, crash/restart, UTC/DST, clock changes, skipped cycles, expiry/revocation; loss isolation; no unauthorized catch-up, policy mutation or real-money order. No withdrawals or transfers automated.


### Guided reinvestment control and interactive preview (TB-08)

Make the Financial Goals reinvestment widget understandable without financial/engineering jargon. The initial state is OFF, 0% and no authorization. Gray out the entire settings and preview panel, freeze editing and prevent pointer, keyboard and API mutations through disabled controls. Keep readable explanatory text and the bottom activation switch accessible; grayscale must not make monetary values or instructions illegible. Red OFF and green ON with explicit text and semantic switch state; do not rely on color alone. The activation switch is the last control at the bottom of the widget and is the sole activation entry point. The red OFF switch stays visually distinct from the gray locked panel. No setting changes or reserve releases are possible while disabled. Display existing protected/active balances as read-only; do not freeze financial data refresh or protective actions.

Provide an interactive, clearly labeled **What happens to my money?** preview with plain-language guidance, using example values only when expressly labeled as simulation, otherwise the current verified portfolio snapshot (paper/live labeled). Show before/after authorized trading capital, protected profit, incremental profit eligible above the floor, amount proposed for reinvestment, amount that will remain protected, hard-cap effect, and next 24-hour evaluation time. A simple stacked bar and exact dollar amounts update immediately when editing the percentage, fixed/percentage/combined floor and ceiling. For illustrative $100 principal and $100 new net profit, $50 fixed floor, 10% explicitly selected example rate and ceiling $125: OFF holds $100 active / $100 protected, ON hypothetical release is $5, leaving $105 active / $95 protected. If the ceiling is $103, only $3 can be released. Never use total account value as the rate base. In the real preview use the exact production deterministic policy evaluator (including prior evaluated cursor, verified balance, caps, earmarks, approval scope, corrections, loss locks and rounding), without mutating any ledger, reserving funds, or granting authority. Show zero and a clear reason if ineligible; unknown facts produce unavailable, not fabricated amounts. A sample simulator is not an approval. Display concise explanatory text for each field, and a summary in ordinary language before the user can approve.

Clicking bottom OFF starts a local, non-authorized setup/preview draft and unlocks editing with actual default rate 0% and required floor/ceiling unset on first setup; saved values may be displayed as drafts on re-entry, never imply authorization. The actual switch remains OFF (red) until valid inputs, preview and separate explicit strongly authenticated bounded policy approval succeed. Pending setup and confirmation are plainly distinguished from authorized ON; cancel, invalid input, stale snapshot, expired scope or rejected approval leaves OFF, with no ledger effect. A green ON switch means a current server-verified approval, not just a client toggle. To change approved parameters, stage a draft and require fresh confirmation; no silent edit of active authority. Switching OFF immediately requests revocation of future releases, retains configured values read-only and relocks/grays the panel after server confirmation; if offline or revocation fails, show pending/unknown rather than false OFF confirmation, with safety locks enforced on the server. Disabling reinvestment never stops monitoring or protective exits and does not automatically move prior releases. No live enablement from paper settings.

Acceptance: authenticated server state is authoritative; verify default OFF/0%, disabled keyboard/pointer/accessibility semantics, contrast and explicit OFF/ON label, bottom switch focus, guided draft and canceled/rejected confirmation, dynamic exact preview with floor/ceiling/zero/unknown and no ledger effects, stale snapshot and policy mismatch rejection, explicit approval/revocation, relock and saved read-only state, offline/restore behavior, and unchanged deterministic capital/risk gates. Financial UI acceptance runs under TB-FIN-01D/E with TB-08/11; do not implement before dependency gates merely to satisfy a visual preview.

## 5. Gate and UI integration (TB-FIN-01D/E)

TB-07 Gate computes allowed new-order stake from available settled active funds minus existing reservations, not total account NAV or protected funds; independently verify at intent admission and order submission. Respect risk caps, min notional, fees, exposure, partial fills, excess costs and recovery. If external movements make the reserve impossible to reconcile, freeze new entries and maintain protection. TB-08 applies same invariants across isolated virtual portfolios and shared venue account. Add an uncomplicated **Financial Goals** dashboard with daily USD and point goals, actual gains/losses, household progress, active amount, protected amount, reserves, estimated tax, operating expenses and explicit reinvestment approval. Show plain-language distinction between protected in-account and externally transferred. TB-09 reports non-sensitive daily progress and harvest alerts; financial mutations not authorized through Telegram. TB-10 requires integrity, backup and secure approval controls. TB-11/12 paper-first acceptance and original forward evaluation; no live automatic promotion. Cognitum later consumes only permitted deidentified market/results snapshots and never modifies goals, balances or orders.
