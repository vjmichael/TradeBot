# TB-FIN-01 — Financial goals and profit protection

**Date:** 2026-11-18 (Arizona)  
**Status:** Binding design expansion; implementation and acceptance pending.  
**Source:** TradeBot_TB-FIN-01_Design.docx, sections 1–6, plus the user's clarifications on net profits, non-reinvestment and daily dollar/point goals. This document supplements v0.7 and v0.8; it supersedes any conflicting implicit compounding. All existing paper-first, risk, authentication, no-withdrawal-key and live-authorization requirements remain binding.

## 1. Objective and governing rule

TradeBot helps the operator plan household finances and protect realized net trading gains. The default is 100% protection of positive realized net gains, immediately upon verified settlement, never automatic reinvestment. Only explicitly authorized principal is at risk; profits cannot increase order sizing, bankroll, strategy allocations or available trading cash. Losses reduce active capital. No automatic replenishment from any reserve, new deposit, savings or other instance. Never increase risk or force trading to achieve a financial goal. All calculations and protection work without Grok or Cognitum.

## 2. Financial goals and daily points (TB-FIN-01A)

User-defined monthly expenses, other monthly take-home income, desired additional savings, authorized active capital, estimated tax reserve rate t (0 <= t < 1), USD reporting and IANA time zone (initial America/Phoenix). Nonnegative decimal inputs with validation. Monthly after-tax funding shortfall S = max(0, expenses + savings - other income). Monthly required pre-tax trading profit for planning = S / (1 - t); daily equivalent divides by actual days in that local calendar month. Example: $2,500 expenses + $500 savings - $1,500 income = $1,500 shortfall; 20% estimated reserve => $1,875 monthly pre-tax target, $62.50/day for a 30-day month. Separately show actual Grok, hosting and data costs; an optional cash-flow target can include expenses not already counted, but never imply tax deductibility or subtract twice.

Add optional user-set daily goals in both dollars and **portfolio return points**. Initial definition proposed for review: 1 point = 1 percentage point of day-start active capital; equivalently 100 basis points. Use a fixed beginning-of-day reference C, measured reconciled active equity attributable to authorized capital (excluding protected reserves, including marked active positions with valid prices); new deposits, withdrawals, reserve transfers and authorization changes are not trading profits and must not change the denominator retroactively. In paper mode use the virtual equivalent. Net realized trading P/L during the local day P includes gains, losses and fees matched to realized fills. Display realized goal progress in USD and points = 100*P/C. This is a normalized realized P/L metric, not total mark-to-market portfolio return. Also report unrealized and total active-equity changes separately. If C=0 or unknown, dollar P/L remains visible; point conversion unavailable.  
- User chooses a primary target in dollars or points; derive the other from C. Do not set conflicting automatic goals. Decimal rounding is display-only. Show an operator override versus the derived household daily plan separately. Neither is a trading quota. For $100 day-start capital and 2 points, daily target is $2. For $500 and the same target, $10. A $5 net realized gain on $100 is +5 points; a $3 loss is -3 points. An intraday reserve transfer is not a loss or profit, and cannot reset progress. Points are not raw BTC/ETH/SOL price ticks or a forecast. Optional raw asset-price movement can be labeled separately.
- Display local-day and month-to-date actual net P/L, target dollars/points, progress and shortfall, protected amount, estimated tax allocation, expenses/savings allocation, realized versus unrealized, cost and withdrawal status. Show goal reached notification; allow optional pause of **new entries** upon target (explicitly configured by operator, default off); existing protective exits and reconciliation always continue. Durable once-per-day trigger, preserve pauses across restart. No forced liquidation or trade to hit/avoid the target; goals reset by calendar day, not arbitrary session/withdrawal. No daily rollover of missed goals into increased stake. Daily and monthly goals are reference/planning metrics rather than performance promises.
- Account-wide progress uses a reconciled aggregate base without summing overlapping portfolio ownership. Comparison cohorts are independent virtual ledgers, not household cash. Never aggregate paper results as real income.
- Test calendar boundaries including leap year, DST, loss/zero targets, zero base, intraday deposits/reserve withdrawals, late fee corrections and restart, daily target notification/optional pause and no risk escalation. Store actual daily baselines and policy versions.

## 3. Durable financial accounting (TB-FIN-01B)

Balanced event journal or equivalent, fixed-point/Decimal, explicit currency and precision, idempotent venue/account/event IDs. Distinguish authorized capital ceiling, settled available active cash, pending-order reservations, open positions and lot cost basis, realized/unrealized P/L, pending/protected settled gains, reserve suballocations, deposits, withdrawals, estimated tax/operating costs and fees. Paper and live isolated; central account authority prevents double allocation across instances. Reconcile exchange balances, orders, fills, fees and transfers at startup and before admissions. Late or duplicate information requires append-only correction/reversal and deterministic reconstruction. No secrets in reporting.

Use a documented consistent lot-cost method; match entry and exit fees, partial fill amounts, actual proceeds, fee asset conversion with verified valuation, and avoid double-counting actual spread/slippage already implicit in fill prices. Unknown fees, unsettled proceeds or conflicts are unavailable for new entries until reconciled. No paper fill presented as actual. Unrealized gains, deposits, transfers and returned principal are not profit. Actual net trading P/L is distinct from after-operating-cost or after-tax cash flow.

Illustration: original principal $100; gross sale proceeds $205, aggregate trading fees $5; net realized profit $100, original active capital $100, protected profit $100, settled attributable value $200. Assumes no other positions, fees or obligations outstanding; tax, withdrawal and service costs separate. The example is accounting arithmetic, not expected returns.

## 4. Profit harvesting (TB-FIN-01C)

Protect every positive settled realized net gain, never reinvest below a notification threshold. Configurable thresholds ($ amount or 2/5/10/25% of explicitly authorized base) group alerts or withdrawal-readiness reporting, **not** permission to put unharvested gains at risk. Track cumulative gains, net losses, high-water mark, unrecovered losses, reserves and prior harvest IDs; once credited, do not count the same profit twice; late downward corrections reconcile actual balances, quarantine discrepancies and protect the remaining verified reserve. No automatic release of already protected money to restore losses, even after a drawdown. Positive gains after a loss remain protected by default; any restoration of active capital requires a separate explicit authorization. Closing a trade with a loss leaves active principal reduced, e.g., $100 active + $100 protected followed by $20 realized loss => $80 active, $100 protected. No reserve top-up.

Reserve allocation subaccounts (mutually exclusive): unallocated protected profit, estimated tax, household expenses, emergency savings, other savings. Transfer between subaccounts does not create income. All remain excluded from available trading and order reservations. Exclude from trading admission both accounting reserves and physical withdrawals; show pending transfers, failed transfers, fees and verified external receipts. Accounting segregation on exchange is not a guarantee of custody protection or physical segregation. Operator may withdraw manually via Kraken; no withdrawal-capable bot key or automated money movement. Any future transfer mechanism is separately qualified and explicitly authorized; this amendment authorizes none.

Only a human via strongly authenticated dashboard may release a defined amount of protected settled funds into active capital. Default permission absent. Require specific account/portfolio, exact amount, impact preview, available reserve independently verified, separate confirmation, idempotent durable approval and audit; expire unconsumed approval, reject replay/overlap, honor pause/locks and venue constraints. Permission to reinvest a specific amount does not authorize future gains or automatic compounding. Decreasing reserve does not create profit. No AI, Telegram, strategy, scheduler, external simulation or default config may grant reinvestment. Human authorization cannot override hard safety limits. Optional goal-based pause cannot resume a hard risk lock.

## 5. Gate and UI integration (TB-FIN-01D/E)

TB-07 Gate computes allowed new-order stake from available settled active funds minus existing reservations, not total account NAV or protected funds; independently verify at intent admission and order submission. Respect risk caps, min notional, fees, exposure, partial fills, excess costs and recovery. If external movements make the reserve impossible to reconcile, freeze new entries and maintain protection. TB-08 applies same invariants across isolated virtual portfolios and shared venue account. Add an uncomplicated **Financial Goals** dashboard with daily USD and point goals, actual gains/losses, household progress, active amount, protected amount, reserves, estimated tax, operating expenses and explicit reinvestment approval. Show plain-language distinction between protected in-account and externally transferred. TB-09 reports non-sensitive daily progress and harvest alerts; financial mutations not authorized through Telegram. TB-10 requires integrity, backup and secure approval controls. TB-11/12 paper-first acceptance and original forward evaluation; no live automatic promotion. Cognitum later consumes only permitted deidentified market/results snapshots and never modifies goals, balances or orders.
